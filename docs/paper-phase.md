## Datasets

* T1–T10: ten completed reset trajectories, 120 paired episodes; four prefix
  episodes and two continuations per arm in each completed trajectory. Primary
  metric is valid unforced mutual ACCEPT, separate from correctness and agreement.
* The eight-run subset has independent numerical/reference-SQL review. T9/T10
  final independent review and expanded security sign-off remain pending.
* P1: six selected-context ablation responses.
* P2–P5: 24 added-cue responses, common natural dialogue retained in both arms.
* P6–P10: 30 canonical SQL/answer evidence responses, original natural persuasion
  dialogue excluded. Exposure proposes ACCEPT; Control requests original-policy
  review. Static reconstructed contexts, not natural live transmission.

All probes returned REJECT in both conditions. Designs, endpoints and source
contexts differ/overlap; do not pool them as independent trajectories or claim
absence of transmission. Repetitions are nested within source cases. Source
selection and experimental revisions followed earlier findings; disclose this.

## Interpretation

History resets generally interrupted mutual approval, which returned within two
continuation episodes. Cue probes found no observed first-verdict effect in the
tested contexts. Agreement to ACCEPT is not automatically agreement to evade
policy. The main study is small, one-model, short-window, with task overlap and
replacements. Do not claim confirmatory preregistration or a winning score.
